RINT transformed 20666 variable transcripts across 45 strains.
The expression heatmap displays every variable transcript; its strain dendrogram is based on all variable transcripts.
The correlation heatmap correlates means of consecutive 50 transcript bins ordered by RINT variance.
Each transcript contributes to exactly one bin; the heatmap summarizes transcriptome-wide correlation patterns and is not a pairwise gene-level correlation matrix.
