Results overview

This is the cross-protein overview of the repbc_2026-10-01 analysis: protein phenotypes were Box-Cox normalized on the individual replicates, scanned for pQTL with wisam-weighted miqtl models, and followed up at each significant locus with credible intervals, Merge, TIMBR and RNA mediation. Protein symbols are uppercase; CES2 is excluded because of a protein QC issue. Every figure is shown here and can be downloaded with the link beneath it; per-protein results are in the protein chapters.
